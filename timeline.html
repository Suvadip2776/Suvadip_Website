Timeline

My academic journey, from 2016 to the present.

2016
  • Began Bachelor in Mathematics (B.Math) at the Indian Statistical Institute, Bengaluru.
2019
  • Graduated B.Math, First Division with Distinction; received the ISI Teacher's Award (top 3 students, 2016–2019).
  • All India Rank-3 in the M.Stat entrance exam of ISI, and All India Rank-33 in IIT JAM (Mathematical Statistics).
  • Began Master in Mathematics (M.Math) at ISI, Bengaluru.
2020
  • Statistical Analyst Intern, Siemens Healthineers, Bangalore (Apr–Jun).
  • Awarded the NBHM Master Scholarship, Government of India.
2021
  • Graduated M.Math, First Division with Distinction.
  • Began the PhD in Statistics at Cornell University, chaired by Martin T. Wells, working with Lionel Levine and Moon Duchin.
  • Awarded the Cornell PhD Graduate Fellowship (one of two incoming PhD students).
2022
  • Professional Development Chair, Statistics Graduate Society, Cornell (Jan–Dec); Graduate Ambassador, Cornell CIS DEI (Jan–May).
  • Teaching assistant for STSCI 4090, Theory of Statistics.
  • Poster presentation at the Hyundai Vision Conference, Seoul, South Korea.
2023
  • Teaching assistant across the year for MATH 6710, STSCI 2100 (Winter & Summer), MATH 4720, STSCI 5640, and CS 6386.
2024
  • Completed the Master in Statistics at Cornell (GPA 3.93/4).
  • Contributed talk and Session Chair at IISA 2024, Kerala; invited talk at the Cornell Statistics Graduate Student Seminar.
  • Awarded the NSF Travel Award and the Cornell Graduate School Research Travel Grant.
  • Posted preprints on the Birkhoff polytope and on quantitative relaxations of Arrow's axioms.
2025
  • Summer Fellow, Data and Democracy Lab, Cornell Brooks School of Public Policy, advised by Moon Duchin.
  • Contributed talk at the Joint Mathematical Meeting, Seattle; poster at Social Choice: Theory and Computation, Wellesley College; invited talk at the Fairness in Social Choice Workshop, Northwestern.
  • Became Research Lead of the Cornell AI Alignment Club (Sep 2025–present).
  • Teaching assistant for MATH 6720, STSCI 4100, and MATH 6710. Awarded the Tapia Conference Travel Award.
2026
  • "EigenBench" accepted to ICLR 2026 as an Oral Presentation (~top 1%); new workshop paper on sortition-weighted RLHF at the ICML 2026 Pluralistic Alignment Workshop.
  • Invited talks at the Alignment Research Center (Berkeley) and IASEAI (Paris).
  • PiTech Impact Fellow, NYC Council Data Team; teaching assistant for MATH 2940.
  • Received the ICML 2026 Silver Reviewer Award and the Cornell Bowers CIS Social Impact and Service Award.
  • On the academic and industry job market for Fall 2026; PhD expected Spring 2027.
